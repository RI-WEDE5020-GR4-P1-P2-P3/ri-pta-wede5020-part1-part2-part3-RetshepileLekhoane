About Longtom Trails Conservancy

What started as a small group of local hikers in 2022 has grown into a leading force in the protection of the Mpumalanga Escarpment's natural heritage.

Our history

What began as an informal WhatsApp group organising weekend clean-up hikes has grown into a registered non-profit with over 40 active volunteers. Longtom Trails Conservancy is dedicated to preserving the escarpment's trail network from erosion, littering, and unsafe off-road use, while keeping trails open, safe, and well-mapped for the hikers, trail runners, and mountain bikers who love this landscape as much as we do.

Our mission

Our mission is to protect the trails of the Mpumalanga Escarpment for future generations. We believe that everyone should have access to safe and well-maintained trails, and we work tirelessly to ensure that these trails remain open and accessible to all.

Meet our team

Our team is made up of passionate individuals dedicated to the conservation of the Mpumalanga Escarpment. From our board members to our volunteers, everyone plays a crucial role in our mission.
