About Longtom Trails Conservancy

What started as a small group of local hikers in 2022 has grown into a leading force in the protection of the Mpumalanga Escarpment's natural heritage.

Our history

What began as an informal WhatsApp group organising weekend clean-up hikes has grown into a registered non-profit with over 40 active volunteers. Longtom Trails Conservancy is dedicated to preserving the escarpment's trail network from erosion, littering, and unsafe off-road use, while keeping trails open, safe, and well-mapped for the hikers, trail runners, and mountain bikers who love this landscape as much as we do.

Our mission

Our mission is to protect the trails of the Mpumalanga Escarpment for future generations. We believe that everyone should have access to safe and well-maintained trails, and we work tirelessly to ensure that these trails remain open and accessible to all.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Register on our volunteer form. Clean-up hikes take place on the first Saturday of every month at 07:30, starting from the Lydenburg Trailhead Office.

No, volunteering is free. Please bring water, sun protection and sturdy shoes. We provide gloves, bags and tools.

Our sponsorship packages start at R1,500 for a Trail Marker Sponsor and go up to R15,000 for a Trail Champion. See the sponsorship form for what each package covers.

The Dullstroom Wetland Trail is a flat, family-friendly boardwalk and is our easiest route. Use the difficulty filter on our trails page to find a route that suits your group. The Mac Mac Falls Ridge is for experienced hikers only.

Send us a message on the contact page and choose "Report a trail problem". Tell us which trail it is and roughly where the problem is.
